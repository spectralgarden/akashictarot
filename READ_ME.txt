Akashic Tarot Independent Study Guide

The complete card directory is stored as semantic HTML in index.html. Keeping
the entries in the page makes them readable to search crawlers and visitors
even when JavaScript is unavailable. Each entry contains a concise study note
and a reflection prompt; the decorative suit/card icons are hidden from
assistive technology.

app.js progressively enhances the static directory with:
- Search across card titles, notes, and prompts.
- Combined search and suit filters, with accessible result announcements.
- A random-card action limited to the currently visible results.
- Reduced-motion-aware scrolling and focus highlights.

To update an entry, edit its article in index.html and retain its card title,
data-category, card note, and reflection prompt. The directory contains 22
Major Arcana entries and 10 entries for each of Scrolls, Roses, Forces, and
Keys. Search and filters require JavaScript; the full directory does not.

The guide is an independent educational reference, not an official source or a
replacement for the deck's guidebook. Check attribution, permissions, and
content rights before publishing or changing deck-related material.
